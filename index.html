Research

Proof as a foundation for trust

My research lies at the intersection of software engineering and formal methods. Following early work on proof assistants — Coq, now Rocq, and Focalize — I now focus primarily on automated deduction in first-order logic and reasoning modulo theories.

My goal is twofold: to design efficient proof-search methods and to assess them through concrete tools and reproducible benchmarks.

01

Automated deduction

Concurrent tableaux, deduction modulo theory, superdeduction and proof certification.

02

Software dependability

Modelling and verification of critical systems, program proofs and certified compilation.

03

Type theory

Proof assistants, inductive relations, tactic languages and type isomorphisms.

04

Theories & computation

Set theory, arithmetic, geometry and interactions between deduction and computer algebra.

My training was guided by Jean-Marie Ohl, a guide with the Association Internationale des Guides du Mont Blanc in Chamonix. My expedition experience was developed with Jean Annequin, a French mountain guide and expedition specialist.
